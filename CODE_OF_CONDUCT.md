# Code of conduct

ShowKit contributors must make participation respectful, safe, and
collaborative.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical disagreements without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and consent, especially around product captures and security
  reports.
- Credit other people's work and follow its license.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this policy is
violated. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not
include unrelated sensitive information in the report.
